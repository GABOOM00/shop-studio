Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Shop Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (user.info.basic) and uses the video.upload permission only to send the owner's own posts to their TikTok inbox as drafts. The token is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never shared.

The images of each post are published temporarily on this website so that TikTok can download them; only the most recent posts are kept.

No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.
